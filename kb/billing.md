# Refunds
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after approval. They return to the original payment method. To request one, go to Settings > Billing > Request refund, or contact support within 30 days of the charge.

# Invoices
Invoices are available in Settings > Billing > Invoices. You can download each one as a PDF. To add a company name or tax ID, edit Billing details before the invoice is generated.

# Duplicate or unexpected charges
If you see a duplicate charge, it is often a temporary authorization hold that drops off within 3-5 business days. If it remains after that, contact support with the date and amount and we will investigate.

# Plans and billing cycle
You can switch between monthly and annual billing in Settings > Billing > Plan. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated charges. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date.

# Failed payments
If a card is declined, check the card details and that the bank allows online payments. We retry failed payments automatically up to 3 times over 7 days. You can update your card in Settings > Billing > Payment method.

# Price increases
Your price can go up if your plan changed, you added seats or add-ons, a promotional or trial discount ended, or taxes changed. Upgrades are charged prorated immediately. Check the line items in Settings > Billing > Invoices to see what changed. If the amount still looks wrong, contact support with the invoice date.
