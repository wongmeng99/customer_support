# Password reset
Use 'Forgot password' on the login screen. A reset link is emailed and expires after 1 hour. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

# Changing email
Email changes require verification. A confirmation link is sent to the new address, and a notice goes to the old one. The change is complete after you click the link.

# Two-factor authentication
Enable 2FA in Settings > Security. If you lose your device, use a backup code. If you have none, contact support for identity verification.

# Deleting your account
Go to Settings > Account > Delete account. Deletion is permanent after a 14-day grace period during which you can log in to cancel it.

# Locked account
After 5 failed login attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes. Resetting your password unlocks it immediately.
