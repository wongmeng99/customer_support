# Password reset
Use 'Forgot password' on the login screen. A reset link is emailed and expires after 1 hour. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

# Changing email
Email changes require verification. A confirmation link is sent to the new address, and a notice goes to the old one. The change is complete after you click the link.

# Two-factor authentication
Enable 2FA in Settings > Security. If you lose your device, use a backup code. If you have none, contact support for identity verification.

# Deleting your account
Go to Settings > Account > Delete account. Deletion is permanent after a 14-day grace period during which you can log in to cancel it.

# Locked account
After 5 failed login attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes. Resetting your password unlocks it immediately.

# Adding teammates
Go to Settings > Team > Invite member, enter their email, and choose a role. They receive an invitation link by email. Pending invitations can be resent or revoked from the same page.

# Verification email not received
Check spam and promotions folders and confirm the email address is spelled correctly. In Settings > Account, choose Resend verification email. Links expire after 1 hour. If it still doesn't arrive, add our sender address to your safe list or contact support.

# Changing your username
Go to Settings > Profile > Username, enter the new name, and save. Usernames must be unique, and you can change yours once every 30 days.

# Can't log in
If you can't log in, use 'Forgot password' on the login screen. After 5 failed attempts the account locks for 15 minutes; resetting your password unlocks it immediately. If the reset email never arrives, check spam, confirm you used your account email, and request a new link (links expire after 1 hour). If it still doesn't arrive, contact support for identity verification.
